# How to Complete the Status Report

Use `template.md` for each reporting period. Replace every item in square brackets with project-specific information, and remove any guidance text that does not apply.

## General guidance

- Be concise: focus on outcomes, not a diary of activities.
- Use plain language and include dates, owners, and measurable results where possible.
- Keep the reporting period consistent across all sections.
- If a section does not apply, write **None** rather than leaving it blank.
- Link to tickets, dashboards, documents, or other evidence when available.

## Section-by-section instructions

### Header

Enter the project or workstream name, the start and end dates covered, the report owner, and the submission date.

### Summary

Write two or three sentences that give a reader the overall status. Mention the most important accomplishment, the current health of the work, and whether the outlook has changed.

### Accomplishments

List meaningful outcomes completed during the reporting period. Start with a strong verb and include the result or impact. For example, write “Reduced build time by 20%” rather than “Worked on build optimization.”

### Work in Progress

For each active item, state what is being done, its current state, the next milestone, and the expected completion date. Include an owner when the owner is not obvious.

### Blockers and Risks

Describe anything that could delay or negatively affect the work. Explain the impact, identify the owner, and state the mitigation or help needed. Distinguish current blockers from potential future risks when useful.

### Metrics and Evidence

Add the most relevant measures of progress or quality. Use the notes column to provide context, define the metric, or link to supporting evidence. Include a target or comparison so the value has meaning.

### Next Steps

List the highest-priority actions for the next reporting period. Each action should have an owner and due date.

### Decisions and Support Needed

Record decisions that require input, approvals, resources, or escalation. Name the decision owner and deadline. If no help is needed, write **None**.

## Final review checklist

Before sharing the report, confirm that:

- The reporting period and submission date are correct.
- Completed work is separated from work still in progress.
- Blockers include impact and an actionable response.
- Metrics include targets, comparisons, or useful context.
- Every next step has an owner and due date.
- The report can be understood by someone who was not involved in the day-to-day work.
